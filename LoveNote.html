For Madison

From Luciano

Dear Madison,

First And foremost, Happy Birthday. Today is about celebrating you, but I also wanted to take a moment to tell you how grateful I am that you’re in my life.

Ever since I met you, I’ve always been interested in you—not just because of who you are, but because I genuinely wanted to understand you as a person. There were moments when some of your actions were difficult for me to understand or look past, and there were definitely times when a few flags were raised.

But, regardless of all of that, I never really understood why I continued to find myself drawn toward you. No matter how much I tried to convince myself otherwise, there was always something about you that kept my interest.

I tried so hard not to fall in love with you. I told myself to be careful, to take a step back, and not let my feelings get too deep. But, in the end, all of that effort was in vain, because I fell for you just as hard as I was afraid I would.

And honestly, I’m glad I did.

What surprised me the most was that when we actually started talking, I didn’t feel the fear I had expected to feel. Instead, I felt safe with you. I felt comfortable being myself around you, and that is something I had never really experienced before.

There was something about talking to you that made me feel like I didn’t have to pretend to be anyone else. I could simply be Luciano, and somehow, that was enough.

You are such a special person to me, Madison. In so many ways, you are what I had been looking for without even realizing it.

I love your kind personality, your sense of humor, the way you make me laugh, and even the little speech antics you have with certain sayings. Those little things might seem insignificant to you, but they’re exactly the kinds of things I find myself remembering and smiling about.

I could sit here for hours trying to write down every single thing I love about you, but I think I’d eventually run out of space before I ever ran out of things to say.

And honestly, there are some things I’d rather tell you in person, looking into your beautiful eyes, instead of hiding behind a screen and putting them into a letter.

That’s how much you mean to me. You’re someone I don’t just want to write about—I want to experience these moments with you, make memories with you, and be there to tell you how I feel while I’m standing right beside you.

And on your birthday, I just want you to know how grateful I am that I met you, and how happy I am that I allowed myself to fall for you.

I Love You. Forever yours ❤️

— Luciano ❤️
